3D Insight Mode - Interactive Demo

Welcome to 3D Insight Mode!

This is a standalone demo of the 3D Insight Mode application. Look at the bottom-right corner of your screen to see the virtual smartphone interface.

How to Use:

  • Mouse: Click and drag to rotate the 3D model
  • Scroll: Zoom in and out
  • Touch: Drag with one finger to rotate, pinch to zoom
  • Drag: Click and drag the top bar to move the smartphone
  • Minimize: Click the minimize button (−) to collapse

Available Geometry Types:

  • Cube / Box
  • Sphere
  • Cylinder
  • Cone
  • Pyramid
  • Torus
  • Platonic Solids (Dodecahedron, Icosahedron, Octahedron, Tetrahedron)
  • Prism (n-sided)
  • Custom geometries with vertices and faces

Integration with Moodle

In a production environment, this application integrates seamlessly with Moodle LMS to fetch problem data and track student progress. The smartphone interface displays 3D geometry problems that students can rotate and explore from all angles.
